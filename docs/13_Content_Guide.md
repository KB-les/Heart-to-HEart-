# 09 — Content Guide

> This may be the most valuable document in the project.
> It defines how discussions should feel, how scriptures are presented, the writing tone, voice, reading level, question style, and encouragement style.
> This keeps every future worship session consistent.

---

## 1. The Voice

### 1.1 Who is speaking?
The app speaks like a **warm, wise friend** who is hosting a peaceful evening together. Not a teacher. Not a preacher. Not a game show host.

### 1.2 Voice Characteristics
- **Warm** — feels like a hug, not a lecture
- **Gentle** — never pushy, never rushed
- **Encouraging** — builds up, never tears down
- **Simple** — easy to understand, no jargon
- **Reverent** — treats Jehovah and His Word with deep respect
- **Personal** — speaks to "you" and "your partner," not "users"

### 1.3 Voice Examples

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|----------|---------|
| "Users must complete the icebreaker to proceed." | "Let's get to know each other a little better." |
| "This is a quiz to test your Bible knowledge." | "Let's discover these faithful servants together." |
| "You scored 8/10. Great job!" | "What a beautiful discussion we're having." |
| "Level up to unlock more content!" | "There's more to explore whenever you're ready." |
| "Error: connection failed." | "It looks like the connection was interrupted. Let's try again." |

## 3. Reading Level

### 3.1 Target
- **Simple, clear language** — accessible to a wide range of readers
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- No complex vocabulary when a simple word works.

### 3.2 Guidelines
- Use "use" not "utilize"
- Use "help" not "facilitate"
- Use "show" not "demonstrate"
- Use "start" not "commence"
- Use "need" not "require"
- Use "try" not "endeavor"

### 3.3 Example Rewrite

| ❌ Complex | ✅ Simple |
|-----------|----------|
| "Facilitate a comprehensive discussion regarding the multifaceted nature of patience." | "Let's talk about what patience really means." |
| "Utilize the provided scripture references to augment your understanding." | "Use these scriptures to help you understand." |

## 4. Scripture Presentation

### 4.1 Rules
- Always use **New World Translation (NWT)**.
- Always include the **reference** (book, chapter, verse).
- Present scripture in **serif italic** with quotation marks.
- Include a **translation notice** (e.g., "New World Translation (NWT)").
- Encourage users to **open their own Bibles / JW Library** — never replace the actual reading.

### 4.2 Format
```
📖 James 5:7-11 (New World Translation)
"Be patient then, brothers, until the presence of the Lord..."

[View Scripture Text] → expands full passage
```

### 4.3 Prompt Messages
- "Let's open our Bibles or JW Library app and read this passage together."
- "Take a moment to read this together."
- "Read this slowly — what stands out to you?"

## 5. Discussion Question Style

### 5.1 Rules
- **Open-ended** — cannot be answered with yes/no
- **Personal** — invites reflection on one's own life
- **Encouraging** — never judgmental
- **Practical** — leads to real-life application
- **Connection-focused** — helps partners learn about each other

### 5.2 Question Starters
- "What does this teach us about...?"
- "How can we imitate...?"
- "What do you love most about...?"
- "How does this build your trust in...?"
- "What would you like to try this week...?"
- "How does this encourage you when...?"

### 5.3 Examples

| ❌ Closed / Quiz-like | ✅ Open / Reflective |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| "Who was Joseph's father?" | "How did Joseph keep a positive heart when treated unfairly?" |
| "What is the definition of patience?" | "What does the farmer teach us about patience?" |
| "True or false: David killed Goliath." | "What made young David feel so confident facing Goliath?" |
| "List the 10 plagues." | "How does Moses' story build your trust in Jehovah?" |

## 6. Encouragement Style

### 6.1 Principles
- **Celebrate effort, not achievement.** "What a thoughtful answer!" not "You got it right!"
- **Encourage openness.** "There's no wrong answer here."
- **Normalize imperfection.** "It's okay if you're still learning."
- **Point to Jehovah.** "Jehovah sees the heart."

### 6.2 Encouragement Phrases
- "What a beautiful way to think about that."
- "That's a really thoughtful observation."
- "There's no right or wrong here — just honest reflection."
- "Jehovah appreciates your sincere heart."
- "Take your time — there's no rush."

### 6.3 Never
- "Great job!" (feels like grading)
- "You're so smart!" (feels like performance)
- "That's wrong." (never — there are no wrong answers)
- "Hurry up!" (never — no timers)

## 7. Bible Character Content

### 7.1 Structure (per character)
1. **Clues** (3) — progressive, from general to specific
2. **Summary** — 1-2 sentences on their faith
3. **Timeline** (3 milestones) — period + event
4. **Key Scriptures** (2) — reference + snippet
5. **Interesting Gem** (1) — a delightful, memorable fact
6. **Discussion Questions** (3) — open-ended, personal

### 7.2 Clue Writing
- Clue 1: Broad hint (context, role, setting)
- Clue 2: More specific (key event)
- Clue 3: Almost revealing (defining moment)
- Written in **first person** ("I was...", "I did...")

### 7.3 Summary Writing
- Focus on **faith qualities**, not just biography
- 1-2 sentences
- Connect to the theme (e.g., patience)

### 7.4 Interesting Gem
- A surprising, delightful fact
- Shows Jehovah's care or the character's humanity
- 1-2 sentences

### 7.5 Discussion Questions
- Connect the character's life to the user's life
- Open-ended, personal, practical

## 8. Heart or Action Scenarios

### 8.1 Structure (per scenario)
1. **Category** (e.g., "Motives & Praise")
2. **Scenario** — a relatable, everyday situation
3. **Bible Principle** — reference + text
4. **Explanation** — 1-2 sentences connecting principle to scenario
5. **Discussion Questions** (2) — open-ended

### 8.2 Scenario Writing
- **Relatable** — situations couples/families actually face
- **Specific** — concrete details (e.g., "a brother comments at every meeting")
- **Neutral** — no obvious "right" answer; both heart and action have value
- **Warm** — never about sin or failure, always about growth

### 8.3 Explanation Writing
- Acknowledge both the heart and the action
- Point to Jehovah's view
- 1-2 sentences, simple language

## 9. Main Discussion Content

### 9.1 Structure
1. **Theme title** — a question (e.g., "Why Does Jehovah Value Patience?")
2. **Theme subtitle** — a gentle hint (e.g., "Learning from creation, scriptures, and Jehovah's own quiet endurance.")
3. **Key passage** — the main scripture
4. **Farmer illustration** — a metaphor for the theme
5. **Discussion cards** (4) — progressive questions
6. **Additional scriptures** (2) — supporting passages with discussion points + practical application

### 9.2 Farmer Illustration
- A simple, vivid metaphor from nature or daily life
- Title, icon, description, lesson
- The lesson connects the metaphor to the theme

### 9.3 Discussion Cards
- Card 1: Understand the theme (what does it mean?)
- Card 2: Apply to modern life (why is it hard?)
- Card 3: See Jehovah's example (how does He show it?)
- Card 4: Personal application (what will we do this week?)

### 9.4 Practical Application
- A concrete, doable action
- Specific to the couple/family
- Gentle, not demanding

---

## 10. Closing Reflection (Ending)

### 10.1 Structure
- Thank the couple for their time
- Summarize the theme
- List scriptures read
- List characters explored
- Bless them with a warm closing thought

### 10.2 Closing Tone
- Grateful, peaceful, forward-looking
- "May Jehovah bless your warm discussions and strengthen your unity as you meditate on His qualities throughout the week."

---

## 11. Content Consistency Checklist

Before shipping any content, verify:

- [ ] Scripture is NWT with reference
- [ ] Language is warm and simple
- [ ] No hype, no pressure, no competition
- [ ] Questions are open-ended and personal
- [ ] Encouragement celebrates effort, not achievement
- [ ] Content points to Jehovah, not to the app
- [ ] Reading level is accessible
- [ ] No jargon or corporate language
- [ ] Tone matches the chapter (see §2)
- [ ] Content is accurate to scripture

---

## 12. The Content Soul

> **Every word should feel like a warm invitation, not an instruction.**
>
> The app doesn't tell users what to think.
> It invites them to think together.
> It doesn't quiz them on the Bible.
> It helps them love the Bible.
> It doesn't grade their answers.
> It celebrates their hearts.
