# 01 — Project Vision

## Why does this app exist?

**Heart to Heart** exists to help couples and families grow closer to Jehovah — and to each other — through meaningful, guided Bible discussions.

It is not a quiz app. It is not a game with scores. It is a **worship companion** that turns a quiet evening into a warm, memorable spiritual experience.

## The Problem

Many families want to have meaningful family worship, but struggle with:

- **Where to start** — blank page, no structure
- **Keeping it engaging** — reading alone feels dry
- **Staying on topic** — conversations drift
- **Remote connection** — couples who are apart can't worship together easily
- **Reusability** — every session feels like starting from scratch

## The Solution

Heart to Heart is a **Family Worship Engine** — a platform where each worship session is a **content package** loaded into a beautiful, reusable experience engine.

The engine handles:

- The journey flow (7 chapters)
- The animations and atmosphere
- The turn-based interactions
- The remote sync (WebRTC)
- The sound design
- The progress tracking

The content package defines:

- The theme (e.g., Patience, Love, Faith)
- The opening scripture
- The icebreaker questions
- The Bible characters to explore
- The discussion cards
- The closing reflection

## The Vision

> **Heart to Heart is not an app. It is a platform that can grow with a family for years.**

Today: Patience.
Next week: Loyal Love.
Next month: The Beatitudes.
Next year: A library of worship experiences.

## Core Values

1. **Spiritual first** — Every feature points back to Jehovah and His Word.
2. **Conversation over competition** — No scores, no winners, no losers.
3. **Beauty over complexity** — Peaceful, warm, storybook aesthetics.
4. **Reusable by design** — The engine is theme-agnostic.
5. **Accessible to all** — Simple language, gentle pacing, no pressure.

## Success Metrics

- A couple completes a full worship session together
- They have at least one meaningful conversation they wouldn't have had otherwise
- They return for a second session with a different theme
- They feel closer to Jehovah and to each other afterward

## Non-Goals (v1)

- No user accounts / authentication
- No backend database
- No social features
- No monetization
- No competitive scoring
- No push notifications

## Target Users

- **Primary:** Karabelo & Yolanda (the founding couple)
- **Secondary:** Other couples and families who value Bible-based worship
- **Future:** Any family that wants a guided, beautiful worship experience
