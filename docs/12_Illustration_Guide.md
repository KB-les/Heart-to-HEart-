# 12 — Illustration Guide

> Consistent art style. No random AI styles.
> Every Bible character should feel like they belong in the same world.
> Landscape style. Warm light. Painterly. Storybook.

---

## 1. Art Style Definition

**"Warm Storybook"** — The art style evokes a beautifully illustrated children's Bible storybook.

### 1.1 Core Characteristics
- **Landscape orientation** — scenes feel expansive and peaceful
- **Warm light** — golden hour, soft sunrises, gentle glows
- **Painterly** — soft edges, blended colors, not sharp vector
- **Storybook** — charming, approachable, not photorealistic
- **Nature-rich** — trees, fields, skies, water as recurring elements

### 1.2 What It's NOT
- Not photorealistic
- Not flat vector / corporate
- Not dark or moody
- Not cartoonish / childish (though it can be playful)
- Not random AI styles

## 5. Illustration Rules

1. **Consistency.** All art must feel like it belongs in the same world.
2. **Warm light.** Golden hour lighting preferred.
3. **Nature-rich.** Include natural elements (trees, fields, skies).
4. **Peaceful mood.** No harsh, dark, or chaotic scenes.
5. **Approachable.** Not intimidating, not childish.
6. **Storybook quality.** Charming and beautiful.
